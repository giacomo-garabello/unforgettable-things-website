Delete your sync account

Unforgettable Things for Android, by Giacomo Garabello.

The app itself has no account. Only cloud sync, which is optional and paid, has one, made when you sign in with Google. This page shows how to delete it, with or without the app, and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open Account.
  2. Tap the pencil next to your name, at the top.
  3. Choose Delete sync account, then confirm.

Without the app

Open the deletion page, sign in with the Google account you use for sync, and confirm. It works in any browser, on any device.

Delete my sync account

What's deleted, at once

  • Your account on the sync server: your Google account ID and email address, and your sign-ins.
  • The encrypted copies of the catalogue your phones sent, and the record of your Google Play subscription.
  • If nobody else is left in your household, all its encrypted files and photos.
  • Your Google Play subscription stops renewing. It still runs to the end of the time you paid for.
  • From the app, also the copy of your household key in your own Google Drive. Without the app it stays there, but once the files are gone it opens nothing. To remove it yourself: Google Drive on the web, Settings, Manage apps, Unforgettable Things, Options, Delete hidden app data.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Your catalogue on your phones. It never depended on the account, so the app keeps working without sync.
  • In a household, what you shared stays with the other members.
  • The sync server's database can be restored to an earlier moment for up to 30 days, a safeguard of its host, Cloudflare. After 30 days, the deleted account is gone from that history too. Deleted files aren't kept.
  • The server's logs never hold your data: only which kind of request came in, its result and its size.
  • Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases, under Google's privacy policy.
